Astir — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026
Publisher: Mariia Velikdanova ("we", "us", "our")
Contact: harborlight-apps@proton.me

Summary (the plain-language version)

  • Astir has no accounts. Your alarms, their times and labels stay on your iPhone.
  • This version sends anonymous usage statistics (random installation code, device model, iOS version, time zone offset, what you tapped, purchase results) so we can fix problems and improve the app. You can switch this off in Settings. In the EU, UK and Switzerland we ask first.
  • If you came from an Apple Search ad, this version uses Apple's own attribution to learn which ad it was. No advertising ID is used and no ad-network code is inside this version.
  • We never sell your information. We may add analytics, attribution, advertising and diagnostics partners in future versions (Section 4); we will update this policy and, where the law requires, ask your permission first.
  • You can ask us to delete what we hold about your installation (Section 6).

The rest of this document is the full legal text.

1. Who we are and scope

Mariia Velikdanova is the controller of personal data processed through the Astir app for iOS, our websites and support channels (the "Services"). By using the Services you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the Services. Capitalised terms are used for convenience only.

2. Data that stays on your device

Alarms (times, labels, repeat days, sounds, wake-up tasks), settings, a short local log of recent alarm rings, and your first-run answers are stored on your iPhone. They are not sent to us unless Section 3 or Section 4 says so. Deleting the app deletes this data. Permissions (alarms, notifications, and in the future camera, microphone, motion, location, photos, health data) are controlled in iOS Settings and used on your device for the function you requested.

3. What this version collects and uses

3.1 Anonymous usage statistics (Statistics). When Statistics are on, the app sends to our server: a random installation code (not an advertising or device identifier; a new one is created after reinstalling); app version; iOS version and iPhone model; time zone offset from UTC (not your location); event times to the second in UTC, except that for events about an alarm ringing or you getting up we keep only the date; which screens of the first-run flow you reached and for how long (in ranges); your first-run answers as option numbers, including age range, how you heard about Astir and, if asked, gender or "prefer not to say"; whether you allowed alarms and notifications; paywall views, the plan you looked at and the result of a purchase attempt; whether you created a first alarm, completed a wake-up task or reported a missed ring; and on which days you opened the app.

3.2 Purchases. Apple processes payments. Our server receives subscription notifications from Apple (plan, price, offer, dates, status, country or region, transaction identifier), together with your installation code while Statistics are on. We use them to confirm subscriptions, count revenue, prevent fraud and for tax and accounting.

3.3 Apple Search Ads attribution. If you installed Astir after tapping an Apple Search ad, the app may request an attribution token from Apple's AdServices framework and send it to Apple, which returns campaign information (campaign, ad group, keyword) so that we know which ad worked. This does not use the advertising identifier and does not require tracking permission.

3.4 Support. If you write to us we receive your email address and message and use them to reply.

3.5 Diagnostics. We receive crash and performance reports that Apple makes available to developers (users who share analytics with developers in iOS Settings), and the technical fields in 3.1.

3.6 Server records. We do not store your IP address, and we do not enable access logs that would contain it. Our server records the date (not the time) a batch was received.

3.7 What this version does not collect: your name, email (except in support messages), phone number, contacts, photos, location, the times or labels of your alarms, the time you wake up, free text you type, advertising identifier, or the content of any sound or camera input.

4. What we may add later

To keep this policy stable as Astir grows, it describes categories we are allowed to introduce in later versions. We will not collect or share these until the app version that does so is released; at that point we move the item to Section 3, update the effective date, update the App Store privacy label, and, in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, ask for your permission where the law requires it.

  • Analytics and diagnostics providers (for example product analytics, session or screen-flow analysis, crash and performance reporting) acting for us as processors.
  • Attribution and measurement partners and advertising networks, including mobile measurement services and Apple's SKAdNetwork/AdAttributionKit, which receive device and ad-interaction data (for example the advertising identifier where you allow tracking, install and conversion events, campaign identifiers, coarse location derived from your country or region) to measure and improve advertising and to show or limit ads to you.
  • Additional usage data: more detailed events, in-app interactions, alarm settings and schedules, sleep and wake-up patterns derived from how you use the app, device settings (language, region, text size, battery state), network type.
  • Sensor and media features (for example wake-up tasks using the camera, microphone or motion sensors), processed on your device unless we tell you otherwise and ask for separate consent; health or sleep data only with your explicit consent and never for advertising.
  • AI features, which may send content you submit (for example a photo for a wake-up task) to service providers acting for us, only with your explicit permission.
  • Optional accounts, referral programmes, surveys and promotions, collecting an email address or similar information you choose to provide.

5. Legal bases, sharing, transfers and retention

Legal bases (EEA, UK, Switzerland). Performance of a contract (providing the app and purchases); our legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, measuring and improving the Services, where not overridden by your rights); legal obligation (tax, accounting); and your consent (Statistics and any item in Section 4 that requires it), which you can withdraw at any time without affecting earlier processing.

Sharing. With Apple (purchases, attribution, App Store); with service providers acting on our instructions, such as Amazon Web Services (hosting in Europe, region Ireland, or the United States), and the providers described in Section 4 once they are introduced; with professional advisers; with authorities when required by law; and in a business transfer such as a merger or sale. We do not sell your personal information for money. If we adopt advertising partners, some sharing of identifiers or events for advertising may count as "sale" or "sharing" under some US state laws; we will then provide the opt-out those laws require.

International transfers. Data may be processed in countries other than yours, including the United States, using safeguards the law permits where required (such as standard contractual clauses).

Retention. Event records are kept up to 13 months (and a few days until automatic deletion completes), after which only totals that cannot be traced to an installation remain. Purchase and tax records are kept as long as the law requires. Support messages are kept as long as needed to help you and for a reasonable period afterwards. We may keep data longer where necessary for legal claims.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Statistics: switch off any time in Settings → Share anonymous usage statistics. The app stops sending, deletes unsent events and stops passing the installation code to Apple with purchases. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland Astir sends nothing until you choose "Allow" on the first-launch window.
  • Tracking (when introduced): you can refuse the iOS tracking prompt or turn tracking off in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; advertising partners will then not receive the advertising identifier.
  • Access, deletion, correction, portability: Settings shows your installation code. Email it to harborlight-apps@proton.me and we will tell you which records exist for it and delete them on request. We cannot find records without the code because we do not know who you are. We respond within the time the law requires (generally one month under GDPR, 45 days under California law).
  • EEA, UK, Switzerland: you may also object to processing based on legitimate interests, restrict processing, and complain to your data protection authority.
  • California and other US states: you may ask what we hold, request deletion or correction, opt out of sale/sharing, and limit use of sensitive data; we do not discriminate for exercising these rights. We honour recognised opt-out preference signals once advertising partners are introduced.
  • Do Not Track: Astir does not track you across other companies' apps or websites in this version, so a Do Not Track signal changes nothing.

7. Children

Astir is a general-audience alarm clock and is not directed to children under 13 (under 16 where local law sets that age for consent). We do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The age question offers broad ranges and never asks for a birth date. If you believe a child has used Astir, email us and we will delete the records. We do not use data of users under the applicable age for advertising.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access is limited to people who run Astir. No system is perfectly secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. For changes that matter we will change the effective date and, if the change introduces a new kind of collection or sharing, tell you in the app and ask for permission where the law requires. The latest version is always at this address. Continued use after an update means you acknowledge it, except where the law requires your consent.
