Astir — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026
Publisher: Mariia Velikdanova ("we", "us")
Contact: harborlight-apps@proton.me

1. In short

  • Astir has no accounts, no ads, and no third-party tracking or analytics code.
  • Version 1.0 does not send any information about you to us or to anyone else. Your alarms and settings stay on your iPhone.
  • Later versions may offer an optional switch, off until you turn it on, that shares anonymous usage statistics so we can improve the app. Section 3 describes exactly what that would include.
  • We never sell your information and we never use it for advertising or tracking across other companies' apps and websites.

2. Version 1.0: what the app does with your information

Stays on your iPhone. Your alarms (times, labels, repeat days, sounds, wake-up tasks), your settings, your answers to the questions we ask when you first open the app (for example how you wake up, your age range, how you heard about Astir), a short log of recent alarm rings, and a random installation code created on first launch. None of it leaves your phone. If you delete the app, it is deleted with it.

Permissions. Astir asks permission to schedule alarms and to send notifications (for example a reminder before your free trial ends). These permissions are used on your device only. You can change them any time in iOS Settings.

Purchases. Subscriptions are sold and charged by Apple. We never see your payment card or Apple Account details. Apple gives us aggregated sales and usage reports in App Store Connect (for example the number of downloads and subscriptions by country). They do not identify you.

Support. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to answer you. We keep the conversation as long as needed to help you.

3. Optional usage sharing (not in version 1.0)

A later version may add a switch, Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage data. It is off by default. The app asks once, with two equally clear choices, and you can change your mind at any time. Astir works the same and every paid feature stays available whichever you choose. Nothing in this section applies while the switch is off, and nothing is sent before you turn it on.

If you turn it on, the app may send:

  • a random installation code (not your name, not an advertising ID, not a device ID; it is replaced if you delete and reinstall the app);
  • the app version;
  • which screens of the first-run flow you reached and whether you allowed alarms and notifications;
  • your answers to the first-run questions as option numbers (for example "option 3 of 5"), including your age range, and, if the app asks it, your gender or "prefer not to say", and how you heard about Astir;
  • whether the paywall was shown, which plan you looked at, and the result of a purchase attempt (started a trial, cancelled, failed);
  • whether you created a first alarm, whether you completed your first wake-up task, and whether you reported that an alarm did not ring.

We never send: your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, location, the times or labels of your alarms, what time you wake up, free text you typed, or the content of any sound or camera input. Our server does not store your IP address.

Purchase records. If you subscribe, Apple notifies our server about the subscription: the plan, price, offer, dates, status, country or region, and Apple's transaction identifier. If you turned usage sharing on, the notification also carries your installation code, so that we can see which first-run steps lead to a subscription. We use these records to confirm that subscriptions work, to count revenue, and for tax and accounting.

Why we use it. To find where people get stuck, fix wake-up reliability problems, test changes to the first-run flow and the paywall, and measure whether Astir is worth the price. Nothing else.

Where it goes and who sees it. To a server we run on Amazon Web Services in the United States. Amazon processes it for us as a hosting provider. We do not share it with anyone else, except if the law requires it. We do not combine it with data from other companies, and we do not try to work out who you are.

How long we keep it. Individual event records for up to 13 months, after which we keep only totals that cannot be traced to an installation. Purchase records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require.

4. Your choices and rights

  • Turn sharing off at any time in Settings → Privacy. The app stops sending immediately, and events not yet sent are deleted from your phone.
  • Delete what we hold. Settings → Privacy shows your installation code. Email it to harborlight-apps@proton.me and we will delete the records linked to it. We cannot find records without the code, because we do not know who you are. Deleting the app creates a new code, so the old one is no longer linked to you.
  • Access and correction. The same code lets us tell you which records we hold for it.
  • If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland: we process the data in Section 3 only on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time (it does not affect what happened before). You have the right to access, correct, delete and port your data, and to complain to your local data protection authority.
  • If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law: you can ask what we hold, ask us to delete it, and opt out of sale or sharing. We do not sell your information or share it for cross-context advertising. We do not discriminate against you for using these rights.
  • Do Not Track. Astir does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, so a Do Not Track signal changes nothing.

5. Children

Astir is a general-audience alarm clock. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The first-run age question offers broad ranges (the first is "Under 18") and never asks for a birth date. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has used Astir with sharing turned on, email harborlight-apps@proton.me and we will delete the records.

6. Security and transfers

Data is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and stored with access limited to the people who run Astir. No system is perfectly secure. Because our server is in the United States, data you choose to share is transferred to and processed in the United States.

7. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the effective date above and, before any new kind of data is sent, we will ask for your permission again in the app. The latest version is always at this address.
