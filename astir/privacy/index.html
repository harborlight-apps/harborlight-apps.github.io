Astir — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026
Publisher: Mariia Velikdanova ("we", "us")
Contact: harborlight-apps@proton.me

1. In short

  • Astir has no accounts and no ads, and uses no third-party tracking or advertising code.
  • Astir collects anonymous usage statistics to find out where people get stuck and to fix problems. It never collects your name, email, location, or the times and labels of your alarms.
  • You can turn statistics off at any time in Settings. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Astir asks for your permission first and sends nothing until you allow it.
  • We never sell your information.

2. What stays on your iPhone

Your alarms (times, labels, repeat days, sounds, wake-up tasks), your settings, a short log of recent alarm rings, and your answers to the first-run questions are stored on your iPhone. Alarms, labels and times are never sent to us. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Permissions. Astir asks permission to schedule alarms and to send notifications (for example a reminder before your free trial ends). These permissions are used on your device only. You can change them any time in iOS Settings.

3. Anonymous usage statistics

What we collect. When statistics are on, the app sends:

  • a random installation code (not your name, not an advertising ID, not a device ID; a new one is created if you delete and reinstall the app);
  • the app version;
  • which screens of the first-run flow you reached, how long you stayed on each (in ranges such as "5–15 seconds"), and where you left the app;
  • your answers to the first-run questions as option numbers (for example "option 3 of 5"), including your age range, how you heard about Astir, and, if the app asks it, your gender or "prefer not to say";
  • whether you allowed alarms and notifications;
  • whether the paywall was shown, which plan you looked at, and the result of a purchase attempt (started a trial, cancelled, failed);
  • whether you created a first alarm, whether you completed your first wake-up task, whether you reported that an alarm did not ring, and on which days after installation you opened the app.

What we never collect: your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, location, the times or labels of your alarms, the time you wake up, free text you typed, or the content of any sound or camera input. Our server does not store your IP address.

Purchase records. If you subscribe, Apple notifies our server about the subscription: the plan, price, offer, dates, status, country or region, and Apple's transaction identifier. The notification also carries your installation code while statistics are on, so that we can see which steps lead to a subscription. We use these records to confirm that subscriptions work, to count revenue, and for tax and accounting. Payments are handled by Apple; we never see your card or Apple Account details.

Why. To find where people get stuck, fix wake-up reliability problems, test changes to the first-run flow and the paywall, and measure whether Astir is worth the price. Nothing else.

When it is on. Statistics are on by default outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. In those places Astir shows a window on first launch with two equal choices, "Allow" and "Don't allow", and sends nothing until you choose "Allow". In every region you can switch statistics on or off at any time in Settings → Share anonymous usage statistics. Astir works the same either way, and all features remain available.

Where it goes and who sees it. To a server we run on Amazon Web Services in the United States. Amazon processes it for us as a hosting provider. We do not share it with anyone else, except if the law requires it. We do not combine it with data from other companies, and we do not try to work out who you are.

How long we keep it. Individual event records for up to 13 months, after which we keep only totals that cannot be traced to an installation. Purchase records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require.

4. Your choices and rights

  • Turn statistics off in Settings at any time. The app stops sending immediately, deletes events not yet sent, and stops passing your installation code to Apple with purchases.
  • Delete what we hold. Settings shows your installation code. Email it to harborlight-apps@proton.me and we will delete the records linked to it. We cannot find records without the code, because we do not know who you are. Deleting the app creates a new code, so the old one is no longer linked to you.
  • Access and correction. The same code lets us tell you which records we hold for it.
  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland: we process the data in Section 3 only on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time (it does not affect what happened before). You have the right to access, correct, delete and port your data, and to complain to your local data protection authority.
  • California and other US states with a privacy law: you can ask what we hold, ask us to delete it, and opt out of sale or sharing. We do not sell your information or share it for cross-context advertising. We do not discriminate against you for using these rights.
  • Do Not Track. Astir does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, so a Do Not Track signal changes nothing.
  • Support. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to answer you.

5. Children

Astir is a general-audience alarm clock. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The first-run age question offers broad ranges (the first is "Under 18") and never asks for a birth date. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has used Astir, email harborlight-apps@proton.me and we will delete the records.

6. Security and transfers

Data is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and stored with access limited to the people who run Astir. No system is perfectly secure. Because our server is in the United States, statistics are transferred to and processed in the United States.

7. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the effective date above. If we start collecting a new kind of data, the app will tell you and, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, ask for your permission again. The latest version is always at this address.
